Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

The Docket Newsletter ("The Docket", "we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Who We Are

The Docket is a weekly email newsletter for practicing attorneys covering AI tools and legal technology. To contact us regarding this policy, email joe@thedocketai.news.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect only your email address, which you submit voluntarily through the subscription form on this website. We do not collect your name, location, IP address, or any other personal information through the subscription form.

We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or any client-side analytics on this website.

3. How We Use Your Email Address

Your email address is used solely to deliver The Docket newsletter, which is sent every Tuesday morning. We will not sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Processors

We rely on two third-party services to operate this website and newsletter:

  • Beehiiv — our newsletter delivery platform. Beehiiv stores your email address and sends our weekly emails on our behalf. You can review Beehiiv's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare — our website hosting and infrastructure provider. Cloudflare may process your IP address as part of delivering web pages and routing subscription requests. You can review Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

5. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Unsubscribe: Every newsletter email contains an unsubscribe link. Click it at any time to stop receiving emails.
  • Access & deletion: To request access to the data we hold about you, or to request erasure of your email address from our records, email joe@thedocketai.news. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are located in the European Union, you may also have rights under the GDPR including the right to data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

6. Data Retention

We retain your email address for as long as you remain subscribed to The Docket. Upon unsubscription or a deletion request, your email address is removed from our active subscriber list. Beehiiv may retain anonymized records in accordance with their own retention policy.

7. Children's Privacy

The Docket is intended for legal professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the newsletter following any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email joe@thedocketai.news.
