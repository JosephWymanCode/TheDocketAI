⚖️ This Week in Legal AI

ABA Issues First Formal Guidance on AI Work Product

The ABA's Standing Committee on Ethics clarified this month that attorneys remain fully responsible for AI-generated work product under Model Rule 1.1. Using AI doesn't reduce your duty of review — it changes what reasonable review looks like. What this means for your practice: Treat AI output like a first-year associate's draft. Promising, but always needs your eyes.
